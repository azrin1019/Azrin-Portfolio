BEHIND THE WORK

I'm a Civil Engineer based in Kuala Lumpur, currently working as a Project Engineer at Fast Flow (M) Sdn Bhd — a specialist drainage engineering firm delivering siphonic and pressurised rainwater management systems across Asia Pacific.

The majority of my time is spent on-site. My day-to-day involves supervising drainage system installations, conducting site inspections, coordinating structural queries through RFIs, handling testing & commissioning (T&C), and producing the full stack of technical documentation required to get work approved and signed off.

My goal is to bring this site execution mindset into the oil & gas industry. I'm not looking for an office role — I want a site-based position where precision, safety culture, and the ability to solve problems in real field conditions matter every day.
